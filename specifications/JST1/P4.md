## Where the Lean statement differs from the paper

- D1: `∫_T φ_i = m_T` is not formalized (Mathlib has no Lebesgue integral of barycentric functions on simplices); P4-16.1
  states everything else of "= ∫_Ω φ_i ∇·F_h".
- D2: smoothness is C³ on ℝ (15.1), C² on Ω × Ω with Ω open (16.2); "smooth data" in 16.3 is replaced by the bounds it
  implies along pairs and Laplacian edges.
- D3: in 15.5 the production is stated with [w]·[ũ]; [ũ] = H̄[w] is the fundamental theorem of calculus of P3-13.1.
- D4: the PDE is pointwise at (t, x), with derivatives along t and the coordinate lines.
- D6: the spectrum model has wall and interior nodes only, unit coefficients, ε2 = 0 (the model of the paper's proof).
- S1 of the review: 16.2 is stated for an abstract flux; that Ismail-Roe is C² near the diagonal is not stated (G6).
- Not stated: the scaling ε2ρch/μ of `prop:wallJST`(b) ("a scaling, not an estimate"); "r_D of either sign".
