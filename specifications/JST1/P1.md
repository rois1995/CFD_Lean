## Differences from the approved proposal

- P1-1.1 also states the equality cases (pointwise sharpness).
- P1-2.7: the uniform state also has uniform ũ, so the scalar dissipation is zero; written out in the statement.
- P1-4.0 writes the chain rule out (momentum and energy rows of ∇w from ∇v, ∇T), plus the values of w_m and w_E at
  (ρ, v, p = ρRT). The mass row of G is free; the viscous flux does not use it.
- P1-4.3b added (admissible states have w_E < 0).
- P1-5.6 also states nondegeneracy and |T| = CFD_Lean `volume` for d = 3. P1-5.7 (d = 3) and P1-5.8 (d = 2) state
  `prop:fv` on real meshes.
- P1-3.1: added hypothesis: a pair that no split contains has `n_ik = 0` (the assembled coefficient comes from
  the splits). Found while checking the statement; without it P1-3.1 is false.
- P1-3.3: the bracket `D` (ε2 (ũ_i - ũ_k) - ε4 (L_i - L_k)) is any split-independent vector (answer G2).

## Differences from the paper (statement level)

- Interior vertex = `Closed M i` (row i of the boundary mass matrix B = c + cᵀ is zero), obtained from a facet
  pairing (P1-5.0a) or, for d = 3, from CFD_Lean's conforming predicate (P1-5.0b).
- `thm:visc`: `d ≤ 3` explicit. Any weights ω_T ≥ 0 (covers split means).
- `prop:consistency`(a): the free-stream property of the closure is a hypothesis (closure out of scope).
- `lem:splitmean`: G2-G4 (sensor fixed input, `bnd` given, positivity hypotheses).
- `lem:eps2`: "sharp" as best constants (G1).
- Sums over coupled pairs are sums over all k; uncoupled pairs and k = i contribute zero.
