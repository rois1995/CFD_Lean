## Where the Lean statement differs from the paper

- The paper has no hypothesis on the cells in `lem:avg`; P7-22.2 needs split orientation (`SplitOriented`) and a
  sub-tetrahedron side test at shared faces (`ConformT`), and P7-22.4 shows the first cannot be dropped (G8).
- Only the pulling family (equal weights over all pulling splits, as P3-11.8). The Kuhn (neighbour-fan) subset is not
  stated (no explicit rational counterexample; random cells show it can fail too).
- The twisted cube is a single cell (all faces boundary); the failure is stated for facet balance and for the
  boundary formula of `β` (P3-11.1), not for an interior vertex of a larger mesh.
