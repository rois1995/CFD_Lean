## Where the Lean statement differs from the paper

- `lem:dissgeom`(d): exact for quadratic q (D2 = A). The trace identity holds at every vertex, not only interior ones
  (Σ_k n_ik·e_ik = 2d V_i always).
- `thm:closure`: (H1), (H2) are hypotheses of the face rule (they are what "exact for quadratics" gives); w̃_q = w(ũ_q)
  is a hypothesis, made possible by P2-8.0.
- `thm:wall`: the adjoint term is defined as `(K E_f) g_b` per node, the form that has the paper's w-pairing and sums
  to zero over the nodes. `ς = 1`.
- `lem:sstentropy`: "Hessian ⪰ 0 with null space ker T" in first-order form (convexity, monotone gradient, equality
  iff T U_1 = T U_2).
- `prop:sstpde`: only the pointwise algebra of Π (the PDE is not formalized).
- `thm:sst`: the hat scheme enters through its balance as a hypothesis (assembly of the full scheme is item 13, P3).
- `lem:matched`: "uniform (ρ, v, p) keeps p and v" is not stated (it is the free stream of the hat scheme, P2-8.2).
