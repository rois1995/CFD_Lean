# M1: exact-real ideal-gas state and physical flux

## Statements and assumptions

For any natural dimension d, use Cartesian vectors Fin d → ℝ. Physical dimensions are d ≥ 1; allowing d = 0 makes the algebra general and does not assert a zero-dimensional physical fluid. Conservative state U = (ρ, m, ℰ), with momentum and total energy **per volume**. Primitive state W = (ρ, u, p). Let Q(v) = Σᵢ vᵢ². The gas parameter is constant γ > 1.

- p(U) = (γ − 1)(ℰ − Q(m)/(2ρ)).
- U is admissible iff ρ > 0 and p(U) > 0; W is admissible iff ρ > 0 and p > 0.
- C(W) = (ρ, ρu, p/(γ − 1) + ρQ(u)/2).
- P(U) = (ρ, m/ρ, p(U)).
- For an oriented area vector S, q = (m · S)/ρ and F(U,S) = (m · S, m q + pS, (ℰ + p)q).

The sources use a unit normal n and area measure dA; S = A n is our explicit integrated-flux convention for a planar face with a uniform state. This is a proposed change of notation/derived formulation, not a claim that the books use S. No face quadrature or curved-face integration theorem is included.

Targets:

1. p(C(W)) = W.p and P(C(W)) = W when γ ≠ 1 and ρ ≠ 0. These algebraic identities hold beyond the physical admissible domain.
2. C(P(U)) = U under the same nonzero assumptions.
3. C preserves admissibility for γ > 1; P preserves admissibility by definition.
4. For γ > 1, conservative admissibility is equivalent to ρ > 0 and ℰ > Q(m)/(2ρ).
5. The admissible domain is nonempty in every dimension, witnessed by C(1,0,1).
6. F(U,S+T) = F(U,S)+F(U,T), F(U,aS) = aF(U,S), hence orientation reversal and F(U,0)=0.

Addition and multiplication on the right of flux identities mean componentwise operations. The flux uses the same record shape as a conservative vector; its output is not asserted to be an admissible thermodynamic state.

## Domain and trust boundaries

Lean's real division is totalized, including at zero. Definitions are total mathematical expressions. At ρ = 0 or γ = 1 their values have no physical interpretation and no conversion-inverse theorem is claimed. The flux linearity identities are algebraically valid even outside the admissible domain and at S = 0. No unit normal is formed.

This milestone proves finite-dimensional algebra, not PDE existence, entropy stability, positivity of a time update, a numerical Riemann flux, mesh conservation, floating-point accuracy or correspondence with compiled SU2. No SU2 source or specification is a mathematical authority.
