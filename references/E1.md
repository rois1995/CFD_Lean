# E1 proof map

The frozen [one-step scope](../specifications/E1-burgers-step-proposal.md) is pinned by its
[freeze record](../reviews/E1/Scope_Freeze.md). The logical decoder uses Lean v4.36.0 at
`611c117bfaf7b7ae1cfcf246ce6fba31e8f509e9`. A model finite value denotes its signed mantissa
times the indicated power of two. Both signed zeros represent zero.
