## Bibliography

- J. Blazek, *Computational Fluid Dynamics: Principles and Applications*, Elsevier, **first edition, 2001**, ISBN 0-08-043009-0. Local file: `COMPUTATIONAL FLUID DYNAMICS - PRINCIPLES AND APPLICATIONS.pdf` (470 PDF pages). Front matter explicitly says first edition 2001. Printed p. 12, Eq. (2.12), supplies the specific total enthalpy identity H = E + p/ρ. §2.4 pp. 16–18: (2.20) conservative variables, (2.21) convective normal flux, (2.22) normal velocity; §2.4.1 (2.26)–(2.29) perfect gas and conservative pressure. Printed pp. 16–18 correspond to PDF pages 39–41. Unit-normal and energy-per-mass notation must be converted explicitly to our area vector and energy-per-volume notation. Edition-specific publisher/author errata were not located in the initial search; absence of errata is not established.
- R. J. LeVeque, *Finite Volume Methods for Hyperbolic Problems*, Cambridge University Press, first printed 2002; local electronic front matter ©2004, eBook ISBN 0-511-04219-1. Local filename incorrectly says `Equations`: `Leveque - Finite Volume Methods for Hyperbolic Equations.pdf` (580 PDF pages). §§14.1–14.4 pp. 291–295: pressure, energy, Euler equations and polytropic ideal gas. Supporting cross-check; no LeVeque-specific algorithm is implemented in M1.

Toro, *Riemann Solvers and Numerical Methods for Fluid Dynamics: A Practical Introduction*, third edition, Springer 2009, Publisher-resolving DOI [10.1007/b79761](https://doi.org/10.1007/b79761).
