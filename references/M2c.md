## Definitions and derivations

CFDLean/Flux/Roe/Spectral.lean reuses the accepted Roe operator, velocity/enthalpy averages and positive sound speed. A is the accepted operator as a linear endomorphism of the five conservative coordinates. Its shifted action is proved to be N v = b(v) vb + dp(v) vp, with vb = (1,u,H), vp = (0,n,u dot n), b the normal-momentum increment and dp the accepted ideal-gas pressure increment. This connects every spectral claim to the actual accepted two-state matrix.

The generic algebra proves b(Nv)=dp(v) and dp(Nv)=c² b(v) under the Euclidean unit-normal and sound-speed-square relations. These give N³=c²N and the acoustic, contact and shear eigenrelations. The physical interfaces derive c²=(gamma-1)(Hbar-|ubar|²/2) and c>0 from accepted M2b, rather than inserting spectral conclusions as assumptions. The original Roe (1981) eigenstructure and the frozen Blazek/Toro/LeVeque locators are reused; the arbitrary-normal and polynomial-projector proofs are project derivations from the approved contract. No new scientific source, variant or entropy correction is introduced.

The projectors are exactly (N²-cN)/(2c²), I-N²/c² and (N²+cN)/(2c²). Their actions, zero cross-products, idempotence, sum I, ranges and spectral reconstruction are proved. These are algebraic projectors; neither Euclidean self-adjointness nor positive semidefiniteness is asserted.

## Dimensions, completeness and arbitrary tangents

Each acoustic projector range is the span of its displayed eigenvector, which is nonzero because its density coordinate is one. The convective eigenspace is proved equal to the kernel of the two-coordinate linear map (b,dp). An explicit right-inverse construction proves that map surjective, so rank-nullity gives dimension 5-2=3. No eigenspace dimension or completeness conclusion is assumed.

The projectors span the full state space. Distinct speeds follow from c>0 even when a characteristic speed itself is zero. Independence of the distinct eigenspaces and their proved dimensions produce an actual Basis (Fin 5) of eigenvectors. The tangent plane is independently proved two-dimensional and a valid tangent basis is constructed. For every tangent basis, the contact vector together with its shear vectors spans the entire convective eigenspace.

The absolute-speed operator is the approved sum of absolute characteristic speeds times these projectors. For every complete real eigenbasis, with any valid choice of tangents or convective basis, it equals R diag(abs speeds) R-inverse. Here R is the basis coordinate-to-state linear equivalence and its inverse is the state-to-coordinate equivalence. The proof also exhausts the possible real eigenvalues using the cubic relation; it does not assume that an arbitrary eigenbasis has the desired speeds. No eigenbasis is chosen in the definition of dissipation.

## Orientation and domain

Normal reversal negates A and N, swaps the acoustic projectors, preserves the convective projector, negates/relabels the speeds and preserves dissipation. State swapping preserves the averages, shifted operator, projectors and dissipation. These identities are proved for the total algebraic expressions; the physical spectral results require the common gamma > 1, both M1-admissible endpoints and Euclidean unit normal.

Only c or c² is inverted in the spectral formulas. Stationary and sonic characteristic speeds remain in the domain. Geometry still uses the accepted Euclidean dot product, not the default finite-function norm. Integrated numerical flux construction belongs to M2d.
