| M2-A · 10 bindings | `weight_pos`, `weight_lt_one` and `weight_sum` require positive endpoint densities. The weights are the square roots of density divided by their sum, and the right weight is obtained by swapping endpoints. `averages_swap` verifies symmetry. In `variance_identity`, expanding the squared weighted velocity gives the positive correction (γ−1)θLθR‖uR−uL‖²/2 to the weighted endpoint sound-speed squares. `endpoint_thermal` connects these to M1 pressure. For γ > 1 and admissible endpoints the endpoint terms are strictly positive and the correction is nonnegative, including equal velocities. `soundSquare_pos`, `soundSpeed_pos` and `soundSpeed_sq` discharge the square-root domain. See Averages.lean:10–105. |
| M2-R · endpoint recovery and derivatives | `parameter` stores √ρ times (1,u,H), with specific total enthalpy from M2a. `parameter_state` and `parameter_flux` recover the same endpoint and its M1 physical flux at positive density and γ ≠ 0. The latter proof unfolds M1’s actual `physicalFlux`; its target is tested explicitly against that function in StatementChecks.lean. The two maps are quadratic in parameter coordinates for fixed γ and direction. `hasFDerivAt_parameterState` and `hasFDerivAt_parameterFlux` differentiate these maps **with respect to the parameter coordinates** and give the displayed continuous linear maps B and C. The algebraic definitions use Lean’s total division outside the physical regime. See Linearization.lean:38–119,201–265. |

As a cross-check the triangular B has determinant 2z₀⁵/γ. `midpoint_velocity` and `midpoint_enthalpy` identify the ratios with the actual Roe averages. `operator_B` proves the operator action algebraically; `roeMatrix_B` specializes it at the midpoint. `roeMatrix_CB_inverse` composes C with B⁻¹.
