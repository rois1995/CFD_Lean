## Mathematical findings

All declaration names below have prefix `CFDLean.Roe.Spectral` and refer to `CFDLean/Flux/Roe/Spectral.lean` at the candidate.

- **Actual operator and domain.** `A`, `shift`, and `shift_apply` (lines 15–55) use the accepted Roe continuous linear operator, viewed as a linear endomorphism of its five conservative coordinates. The literal row action is reduced to `N v = b(v) vb + dp(v) vp`. This gives a direct connection to the preserved M2b linearization and hence M2a's actual M1 flux derivative. Physical interfaces derive the sound-square relation and positivity from M2b (`sound_parameters`, line 633). Their assumptions are common gamma > 1, two M1-admissible states, and Euclidean unit normal `dot n n = 1`. Some purely algebraic identities hold on larger domains. No desired spectral result is supplied as an assumption.
- **Cubic and projectors.** `shifted_cubic` (93), `projector_algebra` (218), `projector_sum` (232), `projector_range` (254) and the `roe_*` interfaces (644–704) establish the cubic, mutually annihilating idempotents, sum-to-identity, exact eigenspace ranges and spectral reconstruction. The polynomials are `(N²-cN)/(2c²)`, `I-N²/c²`, `(N²+cN)/(2c²)`, in minus/convective/plus order. Their denominator requires c != 0. These are algebraic projectors. Euclidean orthogonality and positive semidefiniteness do not follow from these results.
- **Dimensions are derived.** `minus_range` and `plus_range` (292,301) give spans of nonzero acoustic eigenvectors. `convective_eq_kernel` (326) identifies the middle space with the kernel of `(b,dp)`. `waveMap_surjective` (319) constructs a right inverse from the unit-normal momentum vector and an energy vector using gamma-1 != 0. Rank-nullity then gives 5-2=3. `roe_dimensions` (682) and `roe_projector_ranks` (688) give exactly 1+3+1.
- **Completeness and arbitrary tangents.** `eigenspaces_span` (378) follows from the projector sum, and distinct eigenvalues from c>0 give independent eigenspaces. `complete_eigenbasis` (399) collects their finite bases into an actual `Basis (Fin 5)` rather than assuming diagonalizability. `tangent_dimension` (506) and `exists_tangent_basis` (725) establish a nonempty two-dimensional tangent basis. `convective_decomposition` (516) writes any middle-space vector as its density component times contact plus a shear vector. `roe_convective_tangent_span` (705) quantifies over every tangent basis, including nonorthonormal bases. Together with the proved dimensions and acoustic direct-sum split, this supplies completeness for those contact/shear choices.
- **Absolute-speed conjugation.** `eigenvalue_exhaustive` (421) derives the three possible real eigenvalues from the cubic and a nonzero eigenvector. `absolute_on_eigen` (448) establishes the correct absolute-speed action. `absolute_basis_representation` (468) and `roe_absolute_basis` (711) quantify over an arbitrary finite complete real eigenbasis and arbitrary eigenvalue labels satisfying its eigenrelations. `bas.equivFun` maps states to coordinates, and its inverse maps coordinates to states. The composition is precisely R diag(abs speeds) R-inverse, in the correct order. Completeness of such a basis is proved separately. Dissipation's definition contains no chosen basis.
- **Reversal and swapping.** `roe_matrix_reverse`, `roe_shift_reverse`, `roe_projector_reverse`, `roe_speed_reverse`, `roe_absolute_reverse`, `roe_swap` (728–755) negate the signed operators, swap minus/plus projectors, preserve the middle projector, negate and relabel speeds, preserve dissipation under reversal, and preserve shift/projectors/dissipation under endpoint swap. The existing `CFDLean.Roe.roeMatrix_swap` supplies actual matrix symmetry. These total-algebra identities do not assert physical spectral meaning for invalid states.
- **Stationary and sonic cases remain included.** No physical result divides by a characteristic speed or assumes a nonzero normal velocity. Only the sound-speed separation is strictly positive. `StatementChecks.lean` checks stationary convective annihilation (179), sonic acoustic annihilation (199), oblique-normal dimensions (219), explicit eigenvectors (229), projector ranks (244) and sonic zero speed (253).
