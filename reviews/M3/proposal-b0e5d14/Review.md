## Domain and representation precision

The intended finite conforming embedded tetrahedral-mesh assumptions are sufficient for the proposed finite identities. No additional smoothness, orthogonality, Delaunay property, connectedness or manifold-at-every-vertex restriction is needed for those identities. Geometric validity remains a caller assumption until a separate importer validation result exists.

Carry the following precision into the frozen implementation specification. These clarify the existing intended mesh and theorem meanings and confer no extra scientific scope.

1. Interpret “conforming embedded mesh” through actual node identities. The coordinate realization is injective on used nodes, tetrahedron records do not duplicate a vertex set, and intersections of convex hulls correspond to the convex hull of their common node IDs. Face incidence equals membership in the tetrahedron vertex sets. This prevents distinct labels at coincident coordinates, omitted face incidences or duplicate records from masquerading as a valid embedding. These conditions can be definitions or proved consequences of the selected representation. The required property is their faithful realization.
2. Define the concrete interface set once from the unordered used primal edges, choose an orientation, and enumerate exterior triangles or grouped vertex patches with exact multiplicities. Preserve the tuple identifying the external primal face and owning vertex. Provide the mapping to the generic E/B records as part of M3-G. This makes the proposal's “same S_e and T_b” requirement mechanically explicit.
3. Keep V_i's precise determinant-sum meaning visible in statements. If future prose claims that it is the measure of an actual geometric region, identify the subdivision/nonoverlap result supporting that interpretation. The current proposal explicitly limits measure-theoretic coverage.

No amendment to accepted M2 is justified by this review. No input certificate may stand in for the local/global closure or volume-partition conclusions promised by M3-G/V.
